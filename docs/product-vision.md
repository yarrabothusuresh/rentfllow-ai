# Product Vision

### Problem
US event-rental companies spend significant time handling inquiries, checking inventory, preparing quotes, following up with customers and coordinating operations.

### Solution
RentFlow AI combines rental management with AI-powered sales and operational automation.

### Target Customer
US event and party rental businesses approximately:
* $500K–$10M annual revenue
* 10–50 employees
* 1–3 warehouses
* Hundreds/thousands of rental products

### Differentiator
AI-native rental operations.

### Long-term vision
```text
Customer Inquiry
       ↓
AI Sales
       ↓
Inventory
       ↓
Quote
       ↓
Payment
       ↓
Booking
       ↓
Warehouse
       ↓
Delivery
       ↓
Profitability
```
